Mazaaq Tha: Full Game Mechanics and Changes      One document covering the complete game: mechanics, changes, timeline, insults, reflection pop-ups, and the game-over sequence.         1. Core concept      A whack-a-mole style game. Laughing mouths pop up and the player taps them. A bomb hides among them. Hitting the bomb turns the insults back on the player. The player is not told the joke is bad. They feel it.         2. Core mechanic         Tap to whack the laughing mouths (normal hits).         Bombs look like the normal mouths. Hitting one triggers the bomb sequence.         Bomb sequence: red screen, insult bubbles fly at the player, then the cool-down (on every second hit).         3. Changes made         Speed increases with every bomb hit.         A cool-down comes after every 2 hits, with a pop-up reflection line and a fact.         The cool-down costs time on the timeline: -3 seconds, shown as a pulsing red "-3s". Tapping Continue early costs only -1 second.         Each bomb hit costs -1 second.         High score / personal best board added, to create competition.         Optional emoji interaction added (open option).         Everything else stays the same.         4. Timeline (about 90 seconds)         0:00–0:20 Warm-up: slow pop-ups, no bombs. Builds rhythm.         0:20–0:50 Middle: faster pop-ups, first 2 bombs, first cool-down.         0:50–1:20 Pressure: most hits, bombs more frequent, speed bar climbing.         1:20–1:30 Final push: fastest speed, last bomb. The clock matters most.      All changes happen on this timeline. The timer keeps running during every cool-down.         5. Win and lose         Win: fill the progress bar with 30 normal hits before the timer hits zero. Reaching 100% opens the mystery box.         Lose: timer hits zero. Show "Time's up" with the line: Pehle ruko, phir hanso. / پہلے رکو، پھر ہنسو۔ Then Play Again.         6. Never repeat one until all have shown.
